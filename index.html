02 / READ THE LIMITS

A pattern in a short history is still just history.

Counts, median, maximum, and the current streak summarize only the five values entered. They do not measure a dependable trend and do not predict the next round.

Waiting 4–5 minutes after a 10× result has no demonstrated predictive basis. A past high multiplier does not reveal when another will occur.

About the two-bet example

It is not risk-free. A crash before 1.5× can lose both stakes. A 1.5× cashout on R20 returns R30—merely matching the combined R10 + R20 stakes before the small bet’s result. That does not remove the risk of losing either stake.
